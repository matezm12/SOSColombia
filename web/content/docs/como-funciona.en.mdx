SOSColombia centralizes and verifies public data about the August 10, 2026 earthquake — it doesn't produce its own figures, it cross-checks them against official sources (UNGRD, Medicina Legal, SGC, DANE) and community reports, and always shows where each piece of data came from.

<Callout type="info" title="We are not an official entity">
  SOSColombia is an independent, volunteer-run project — not a registered
  NGO, not a Colombian government entity, and not affiliated with UNGRD or
  any media outlet. See the [full methodology](/metodologia) (Spanish).
</Callout>

## Navigating the site

The site is organized from general to specific:

1. **[Home](/en)** — an overview of the event (magnitude, epicenter) and the list of affected cities.
2. **City page** (`/en/ciudad/[code]`) — local figures, that city's aid points, and its veredas or corregimientos.
3. **Vereda page** — when a rural area has its own data, it's broken out separately from the municipal seat.
4. **[Aid points](/en/ayuda)** — the full directory, filterable by type (shelter, collection point, health, veterinary, blood donation, monetary donation) for anyone searching across every city at once, not just their own.
5. **[How to donate](/en/donar)** — verified campaigns and organizations, each with a trust level. See also [international donations](/en/donar/internacional) if you're donating from outside Colombia.
6. **[Community](/en/comunidad)** — social-media posts about places or needs not yet in the official directory, reviewed before publishing.
7. **Search (Cmd+K or `/`)** — the fastest way to jump straight to a city, aid point, story, or report without navigating the menu.

## How the data is verified

Every figure and every aid point carries its source and its date. When two official sources disagree, both figures stay on record — one is never silently chosen over the other. The full discipline behind that is in [methodology](/metodologia) (Spanish); the technical side (automated jobs, moderation) is in [How it's built](/en/docs/como-esta-construido).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the site's figures official?

They come from official sources (UNGRD, Medicina Legal/INMLCF, SGC, DANE) and humanitarian reports (OCHA, Red Cross), but SOSColombia itself is not an official entity — it's the one collecting, cross-checking, and citing them. Every figure on the site shows its exact source.

### Why are there sometimes two different figures for the same thing?

Because there really are, in reality. For example, "reported deaths" (UNGRD/OCHA, an administrative count) and "confirmed deaths" (Medicina Legal, a forensic intake count) are different processes and can disagree for a while — showing only one would hide that real difference. See [methodology](/metodologia) for open and resolved discrepancies.

### I know of an aid point that isn't on the site. How do I add it?

Use the [suggest a point](/en/sugerir) form. It isn't published automatically — a volunteer checks each submission against its source before it appears in the directory.

### I saw a social-media post about an aid point or a specific need. Does that count as a source?

Yes, and that's exactly what the [community](/en/comunidad) section is for — share the post's link there. Social media is the lowest trust level (level 7 of 7), so these posts are shown separately from the verified directory, never mixed in with it.

### How do I know if a donation campaign is safe?

Every campaign on [/en/donar](/en/donar) carries an explicit trust level, and the page has a dedicated fraud-warning section — for example, that "SOS Chocó" isn't a single organization but a slogan used by at least 5 different actors. Read it before donating, especially for individual campaigns.

### Can I use SOSColombia's data in another project?

Yes — the whole dataset is openly accessible. See [Data & API](/en/docs/api) to download it as JSON or CSV.

### I found an error or outdated data. How do I report it?

Use the same [suggest a point](/en/sugerir) form for aid points, or share a link to a more recent source through [community](/en/comunidad) if it's about a figure. A volunteer reviews it before anything changes on the public site.

### Who maintains this?

A small team of volunteers, with no funding or institutional affiliation. See [methodology](/metodologia) (Spanish) for more on who we are and how we work.
