## Verification Rubric (draft — badge logic spec for the eventual webapp)

**Green flags:**
- Platform-hub inclusion (e.g. GoFundMe's official Trust & Safety-reviewed relief hub)
- GoFundMe Giving Guarantee coverage (refund within 1 year if fundraiser found fraudulent)
- Named institutional affiliation (university alumni group, established pre-existing foundation)
- Independent press coverage naming the organizer
- Transparent, specific stated fund-use plan naming recipients/localities (Camila Franco's campaign qualifies: specific city, specific items, specific delivery mechanism)
- Official bank-published account numbers

**Red flags:**
- Link only shared via forwarded WhatsApp/SMS with no other trace
- "Exclusive victim list / rescue video" bait
- Brand-new anonymous organizer profile with no other activity
- Urgency/credential-harvesting pressure tactics
- No press mention, vague or shifting fund-use description

**Source for red-flag rubric:** colombia.com fraud-alert article (confirmed active WhatsApp/SMS phishing, fake victim lists, fake rescue videos, QR-code scams exploiting this earthquake) + GoFundMe's own fraud-recognition support doc. Also: Semana explicitly warned the public to verify any solicitation claiming to be from "Fundación Siempre a Tu Lado" (a real but currently-uncontactable animal shelter) before donating — a concrete real-world instance of the fraud risk this rubric guards against.
