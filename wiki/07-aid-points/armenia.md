# Aid Points — Armenia (Quindío)

Last major update: 2026-08-14 — social-media pass (X/Instagram/Facebook/TikTok). **Updates the earlier "zero shelters" finding**: a shelter has now been crowd-reported (not yet officially confirmed by the Alcaldía) — see below. The overall picture confirmed this pass: government response has been widely perceived by residents as slow/misdirected, corroborated directly in citizen comments across three platforms.

## Local news outlet
La Crónica del Quindío (cronicadelquindio.com) — active, publishing earthquake coverage directly.

## Albergues (shelters) — CONFIRMED official 2026-08-14 (Pass 2)

- **"Coliseo del Sur" — now CONFIRMED as an official Alcaldía-run shelter**, cross-verified across 3 independent sources this pass: Región Cafetera TV (local broadcaster), @nataliapanezzo, and Alerta Armenia (community-monitoring account) — all describing it consistently as an active operation near barrio La Isabela, **restricted to pre-assessed families only** (not open walk-in), currently requesting colchones, cobijas, almohadas, agua. This resolves the prior-pass "crowd-reported only" status.
  - Original lead: TikTok, Stefanny Castellanos Muñoz (2026-08-14): "🚨ALBERGUE TEMPORAL EN ARMENIA🚨 COLISEO DEL SUR📍. Se requieren colchonetas para las familias que irán llegando." Permalink: https://www.tiktok.com/@stefannycastellanosm/video/7673334781010038023
  - **Note the access restriction** ("pre-assessed families only") when directing the public — this is not a general walk-in shelter, distinguishing it from Pereira's/Manizales's open-coliseum model.
- "Coliseo del Café" — still ruled out (see prior pass finding, unchanged).
- Ground-level corroboration of the underlying gap this shelter is responding to: Facebook, "Emisora Comunitaria Simón Bolívar" (community radio station) posted an individual case (2026-08-13): a resident, "Mrs. Nidia Jaramillo," house collapsed, family "sleeping under some plastics," requesting tiles/plastics/mattresses/staples/wire. Phone: 3234361517. This kind of individual-level unmet need is consistent with "no organized shelter yet" even as the Coliseo del Sur report suggests one may now be forming.

## Government social-media presence — a likely explanation for the information gap

- **Alcaldía de Armenia's official X account (@AlcaldiaArmenia, 16.1K followers)** has NOT posted since **February 14, 2025** — over a year before the earthquake. Confirmed by direct visit to the profile. This explains why X/Twitter searches never surfaced official Armenia shelter/aid announcements — they simply aren't using this channel.
- **Instagram is their actually-active channel** (@alcaldiadearmenia, 40K followers, 5,320 posts, frequent recent activity) — but even there, no shelter/albergue post was found this pass. Their most recent earthquake-related content was about CAM (municipal building) reopening and a traffic/vehicle-impound announcement (Setta), which drew sharp public criticism (see below).
- **Direct citizen criticism, X (Jorge Iván Cuervo R., @cuervoji, ~14h before this pass):** "It's unbelievable that in this situation the accounts of @AlcaldiaArmenia and @QuindioGob remain inactive and without providing relevant information."
- **Direct citizen criticism, Instagram comments on the Alcaldía's own traffic-update post** (https://www.instagram.com/p/Db9EW6dvAs4/):
  - "alejo_jimenez7": "Armenia necesitando albergues y ayudas y el alcalde más preocupado por el tránsito y sus fotomultas." (Armenia needs shelters and aid and the mayor is more worried about traffic and speeding tickets)
  - "dani.hurtado.v": "Y cuando quitan el pico y placa para poder movilizar ayudas??" (When will they lift the vehicle restriction so aid can move?)
  - "vaneduran_": "Eso no nos importa!!! Hay gente necesitando ayuda, aparezcan pues!!!" (We don't care about that!!! There's people needing help, show up already!!!)
- **A resident's public account, TikTok ("Andrea", ~1d before this pass):** describes living in "conjunto Alejandría al sur de la ciudad de Armenia," feeling "totalmente abandonados por parte de las entidades correspondientes," with nowhere to go — and explicitly theorizes why: *"no hay pérdidas humanas solo material y quizás por ello no hay atención mediática"* (no human losses, only material, and maybe that's why there's no media attention). **This directly names the pattern this project already flagged independently** (Armenia's zero-death outcome correlating with less attention/response) — now confirmed as the resident's own lived explanation, not just an outside inference.
