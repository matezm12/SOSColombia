## Death/injury toll — UPGRADED to official municipal source
**cali.gov.co's own "Cifras y datos actualizados del terremoto en Cali" page, last updated Aug 12, 2026, 8:00 PM (most current, most authoritative figure found — municipal government source, not press aggregation):**
- 96 deaths (up from the earlier 95 figure)
- 1,224 injured
- 111 missing
- 88 rescued
- 45 buildings totally collapsed, 35 partially collapsed, 832 with structural damage, 18 under evacuation order

**Note**: other outlets (ABC News, NYT) cited NATIONAL figures of 111-224 deaths for the whole country around the same time — do not conflate those with Cali's own 96-death municipal figure.
