## Building census and vehicle restriction timeline — clarifies an earlier apparent conflict

- **Aug 11 (X, Alcaldía de Pereira, https://x.com/Alcaldiapereira/status/2087364111143186840):** "On August 12, the census of buildings affected by the seismic emergency will begin. Additionally, pico y placa will resume normally. There will be no other vehicle restrictions."
- **Aug 12-17 (Infobae, previously logged in wiki/02-cities/pereira.md):** Alcaldía prohibited private-car circulation citywide, only motos/taxis permitted.
- **Resolution:** these are sequential, not contradictory — pico y placa normalized Aug 11, then a stricter car ban was imposed starting Aug 12, likely as rescue/access needs evolved. Not a data error.

## Comunicado oficial: center of Pereira remains cordoned off

Source: Alcaldía de Pereira X post (Aug 11), https://x.com/Alcaldiapereira/status/2087369441818394857. "Por favor, no ingreses al centro de Pereira" — rescue teams need silence to listen for survivors in collapsed structures; heavy equipment (cranes, dump trucks, ambulances) needs unobstructed access. Center remains under authority control due to structural collapse risk — no entry as pedestrian or vehicle.
