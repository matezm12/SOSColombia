## Veterinary / animal aid — confirmed absent, not just unresearched
No dedicated animal-welfare or veterinary org exists for Quibdó in any source checked. Both the Gobernación's coordination sheet and the Diócesis food bank list "alimentos para mascotas" as one generic item category at their general collection points — not a dedicated org/contact. Other cities (e.g. Valledupar, Cundinamarca) have named pet-specific points; Quibdó has no equivalent.

## City-specific toll — clarifies the Quibdó-vs-Chocó-department distinction
- **Quibdó city specifically: 9 deaths, 116 injured, 6 trapped, 5 missing** (El Tiempo, Aug 11 direct fetch) — this remains the latest city-specific figure, no update found.
- **Chocó department (all municipios combined): 13-14 deaths** depending on date (Aug 13 Infobae: 13 dead/186 injured; earlier Governor figure: 14) — confirms the existing "9 (city) vs 14 (department)" distinction in wiki/08-contradictions.md is correct, not a genuine conflict between two city counts.
- Department-wide (Chocó7días.com via search snippet): 43,178 people / 10,124 families affected, 468 homes destroyed, 3,199 damaged.
- Hospital San Francisco de Asís en Quibdó reported operating at >245% over capacity (Infobae, Aug 12).

## Infrastructure

A stronger claim (stairwells collapsed, "uninhabitable") surfaced only via search synthesis and could NOT be independently verified — treat only the "two buildings damaged" detail as solid.
