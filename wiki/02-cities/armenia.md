# Armenia — Quindío

- **Population (DANE 2026 official):** 311,959 (see wiki/14-context-reference.md)
- **Severity:** red alert per initial press classification, but **confirmed zero deaths** — lighter human-toll outcome than the other 4 red-alert cities
- **Status:** active response — a shelter ("Coliseo del Sur," near barrio La Isabela, restricted to pre-assessed families) is now CONFIRMED official as of 2026-08-14 Pass 2 (3 independent sources: Región Cafetera TV, @nataliapanezzo, Alerta Armenia — see wiki/07-aid-points/armenia.md); calamidad pública declared Aug 12. Government's own social-media response has drawn direct, sharp public criticism (documented in the aid-points file) — the Alcaldía's X account has been dormant since Feb 2025, and its one active Instagram post about the earthquake period was about traffic/vehicle-impound policy, not aid.

## Deaths — CONFIRMED near-zero, not a data gap
- **Armenia itself: 0 confirmed deaths**, per Mayor James Padilla García and Municipal Secretary of Government Carlos Arturo Ramírez, both quoted directly and independently.
- **Quindío department: at least 2 confirmed deaths** — Quimbaya (person trapped in a road collapse) plus, per INMLCF's updated Comunicado 06 (2026-08-14 find), 1 victim listed under "Unidad Básica: Armenia-Calarcá" (Daniel Gutiérrez Arias, 84). **Open question, not resolved**: whether this second death attributes to Armenia city or to Calarcá (a distinct nearby Quindío municipio) — same ambiguity flagged for Popayán in wiki/08-contradictions.md. Armenia city's own "0 confirmed" status (above) is NOT overturned by this, since the mayor/secretary statements remain unretracted, but treat the department total as "2, not 1" going forward.

## Injuries
130-307 injured depending on source/scope (Armenia-specific vs department-wide) — see wiki/07-aid-points/armenia.md for the full breakdown. Universidad del Quindío's internal brigade treated 15 non-serious cases separately.

## Infrastructure damage
- Two debris-disposal sites designated (La Patria sector, Estadio Centenario sector)
- 71 schools under safety verification, classes suspended
- Named affected neighborhoods/buildings: Zuldemayda, La Mariela, Salvador Allende/Salvador Allende Alto, Maranatha building (Calle 10 Norte #18-47, evacuated), Las Veraneras, Villa Ximena, La Villa urbanization (54 apartments/6 blocks, 18cm crack widening in one building)
- El Edén airport operations suspended (per Phase 1 research; not re-verified this pass)

## Aid points
See `wiki/07-aid-points/armenia.md` — a crowd-reported shelter (Coliseo del Sur) surfaced 2026-08-14, not yet officially confirmed. Prior finding of zero official shelters as of Aug 13 stands as the baseline this update revises.
