## UNGRD document repository — genuinely blocked (confirmed via browser, not just WebFetch)
`portal.gestiondelriesgo.gov.co` loads fine via browser but internal navigation (Comunicación page, attempted search) did not surface a numbered informe/balance archive this pass — its site structure resists straightforward navigation, not confirmed as a hard block, just not yet cracked. `repositorio.gestiondelriesgo.gov.co` (their DSpace repository) redirects to a `:8443` port that **returns a real browser error page** — confirmed via direct browser navigation 2026-08-14, this is a genuine network-level block, not a WebFetch-only limitation. **OCHA's Flash Update series (see Flash Update 004 above) is now the practical substitute for UNGRD's own primary documents** — it's fed by the same humanitarian country team and gives cleaner multi-sector figures than press-relayed UNGRD balances.
